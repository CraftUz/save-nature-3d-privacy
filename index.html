Privacy Policy for Save Nature 3D

Effective date: November 2, 2026 · Last updated: November 2, 2026

Short version: Save Nature 3D has no user accounts and does not ask for your name, email, phone number, location or contacts. Your game progress is saved only on your device. The game uses Google services for ads (AdMob), ad consent (User Messaging Platform), app statistics and crash reports (Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics) and in-app purchases (Google Play Billing). These services may collect limited device and usage data as described below.

1. Who we are

Save Nature 3D (the "Game") is a mobile game developed and published by CraftUz ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains what information is processed when you play the Game on Android, why, and what choices you have.

Contact: shaxzodmahamadjonov@gmail.com

2. Information we do not collect

  • The Game has no registration, login or user accounts.
  • We do not ask for or store your name, email address, phone number, postal address, photos, contacts or precise location.
  • The Game has no chat and no way to share content with other players.
  • We do not sell your personal information.

3. Information stored on your device

Your game progress (levels, coins, items, settings, home island and similar data) is saved locally on your device. It is not sent to our servers. Uninstalling the Game or clearing its app data in Android settings deletes this progress permanently.

4. Third-party services used by the Game

The Game includes software from Google. These services may automatically collect certain information from your device. They act under their own privacy policies, linked below.

4.1 Google AdMob (advertising)

The Game shows ads, including optional rewarded video ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game reward. To deliver, measure and limit ads and to prevent fraud, AdMob may collect:

  • the Android Advertising ID (you can reset or delete it in your device settings);
  • IP address (used to estimate approximate, country/city-level location);
  • device information such as model, operating system version, language and screen size;
  • ad interaction data, such as ads shown and ads tapped, and diagnostic data.

Depending on your consent choices and your location, ads may be personalized or non-personalized. More information: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and Google Privacy Policy.

4.2 Google User Messaging Platform (consent)

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, Switzerland or another region where consent is required, the Game shows a consent form from Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) before personalized ads are shown. Your choice is stored on your device. You can change your choice at any time from the privacy option in the Game's settings (where available) or by clearing the Game's app data, which will show the form again.

4.3 Firebase Analytics (app statistics)

We use Google Firebase Analytics to understand how the Game is played so we can improve it, for example which levels are too hard or where players stop playing. Firebase Analytics may collect:

  • an app instance identifier generated for the installation;
  • in-game events, such as tutorial steps, level start, level complete or fail, ads watched and purchases made;
  • device information, such as model, operating system version, language and approximate country;
  • session information, such as first open, session length and app updates.

We use this data only in aggregated form and do not use it to identify you personally. See Privacy and Security in Firebase.

4.4 Firebase Crashlytics (crash reports)

If the Game crashes or has a serious error, Firebase Crashlytics sends a crash report so we can fix the problem. A report may include the crash stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, free memory and an installation identifier (Crashlytics installation UUID). Crash reports are used only for fixing bugs and improving stability.

4.5 Google Play Billing (in-app purchases)

The Game may offer optional in-app purchases, for example removing ads or buying coin packs. All payments are processed by Google Play. We never receive or store your credit card number, bank details or other payment information. We only receive purchase confirmation details from Google Play (such as the product purchased, order ID and purchase token) so that the Game can deliver and restore your purchase. See the Google Payments Privacy Notice.

5. Device permissions

The Game uses only the permissions needed for the services above: internet access and network state (to load ads and send statistics and crash reports), the Advertising ID permission (for AdMob) and the billing permission (for Google Play purchases). The Game does not request access to your camera, microphone, contacts, files or location.

6. Legal basis (EEA/UK users)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalized advertising is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time. Non-personalized ads, analytics, crash reporting and purchase processing are based on our legitimate interest in providing, funding, securing and improving the Game, and on performing the purchase you requested.

7. Data retention

Game progress stays on your device until you delete it. Data collected by Google services is kept for a limited period according to Google's retention policies and the data retention settings of our Firebase project. Crash reports are kept only as long as needed to fix issues.

8. Children's privacy

The Game is intended for a general audience aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child under 13 has provided personal information through the Game, please contact us at shaxzodmahamadjonov@gmail.com and we will help delete it. Parents can also reset the device Advertising ID or turn off ad personalization in the device's Google settings.

9. Your choices and rights

  • Ad personalization: change your consent in the Game, or on Android go to Settings > Google > Ads (or Settings > Privacy > Ads) to reset or delete your Advertising ID.
  • Delete game data: uninstall the Game or clear its app data in Android settings.
  • Access or deletion requests: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal data, or to object to processing. Because the Game has no accounts, we cannot link data to you by name; email us and we will do our best to help, including with requests related to the Firebase data in our project.

10. Security

Data sent by the Google services above is transmitted over encrypted connections. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we use services from reputable providers and collect as little data as possible.

11. International transfers

Google may process data on servers located outside your country, including in the United States. Google provides safeguards for such transfers as described in its privacy policy.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the Game or the services it uses change. The new version will be posted on this page with a new "Last updated" date. Please check this page from time to time.

13. Contact us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or your data, contact CraftUz at shaxzodmahamadjonov@gmail.com.
